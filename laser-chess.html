HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Be the first to hit your opponent's King with the laser beam.

PIECES

  • Laser: Emits the laser beam. Cannot be moved or destroyed.
  • King: Your most important piece. If the laser hits it, you lose. Can be moved like other pieces.
  • Deflector: Has one mirrored side that reflects the laser 90°. Destroyed if hit on any non-mirrored side.
  • Defender: Has a front shield that blocks the laser. Can be destroyed if hit from the sides or back.
  • Switch: Has mirrors on both sides. Cannot be destroyed. Can swap places with an adjacent Deflector or Defender.

TURN STRUCTURE

On your turn, you must do ONE of these actions:

  • Move any of your pieces one square in any direction (including diagonally)
  • Rotate one of your pieces 90° left or right (changes mirror direction)
  • Swap a Switch with an adjacent Deflector or Defender

After your action, you fire the laser. The beam travels from your Laser piece, bouncing off mirrors according to the angles, and destroys any piece it hits on a non-mirrored side.

RESTRICTIONS

  • Red pieces cannot enter red spiral zones (along the edges)
  • Blue pieces cannot enter blue spiral zones
  • The laser can hit your own pieces too!

CONTROLS

  • Click a piece to select it
  • Click a destination square to move there
  • Rotate buttons to rotate the selected piece
  • Fire Laser to shoot after your move

VICTORY!

DEFEAT
